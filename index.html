About Us

Building Products That Matter

Alex Claudio Ventures LLC is an independent software and digital media company headquartered in Kirkland, Washington. We design, develop, and operate iOS applications, affiliate-driven web platforms, and editorially curated content properties across the Pacific Northwest and beyond.

On the app side, we specialize in utility, lifestyle, and AI-driven mobile applications built to the exacting standards of the Apple ecosystem. On the media and affiliate side, we build content-rich platforms that connect audiences with curated products, local experiences, and event bookings — generating revenue through strategic affiliate partnerships, commission-based referrals, and sponsored placements.

Our affiliate marketing expertise is end-to-end. We integrate directly with major affiliate networks and ticketing APIs — including Ticketmaster, Eventbrite, and local business partner programs — to surface relevant offers to our audience. Every affiliate link is tracked with unique click attribution, conversion analytics, and real-time commission reporting, giving us full visibility into performance across channels.

On the content and audience growth side, we manage weekly editorial newsletters, SEO-optimized landing pages, and social distribution strategies that drive qualified traffic to our affiliate partners. Our approach combines high-quality editorial curation with data-driven monetization: we score and rank experiences, write original editorial content, and place contextual affiliate links that deliver genuine value to readers while maximizing conversion rates and partner revenue.

Best in Seattle

Local Guide & Affiliate Platform
Web · bestseattle.co

A curated editorial guide to Seattle-area events, restaurants, and local experiences — built from the ground up as an affiliate marketing platform. Best in Seattle aggregates real-time event data from Ticketmaster and Eventbrite APIs, applies editorial quality scoring, and presents hand-picked recommendations to readers alongside contextual affiliate booking links. Every outbound click is tracked with unique attribution IDs, enabling precise conversion analytics and commission reporting across all partner programs.

The platform also supports sponsored business placements, featured listings for local partners, and a weekly curated newsletter that drives recurring traffic and repeat affiliate conversions. Revenue streams include per-click and per-booking affiliate commissions, sponsored content fees, and premium placement partnerships with local businesses and event organizers.
